Privacy Policy

TokenSense for Claude (Smart Router for Claude) · Effective October 3, 2026

Your prompts never leave your browser because of this extension.

TokenSense for Claude does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything it does happens on your device.

TokenSense for Claude (“the extension”) is an independent, unofficial Chrome extension. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Anthropic. This policy explains exactly what the extension does with data.

What the extension does

When you use claude.ai, the extension reads the prompt you are about to send, estimates how difficult it is, and selects the lowest-cost Claude model that should handle it well (Haiku, Sonnet, Opus, or Fable). You choose how aggressive it is with three modes: Saver, Balanced, or Quality. You can also rate a routing choice with 👍 or 👎, and the extension uses those ratings to adjust future routing for you.

That is its only purpose: choosing a Claude model on claude.ai.

Data the extension accesses

DataWhy it is usedWhat happens to it
Text of the prompt you type on claude.ai To estimate difficulty and choose a model Processed in your browser only. It is not stored, logged, or transmitted by the extension.
Your settings (such as routing mode) To remember your choices Saved on your device using chrome.storage.
Your 👍/👎 ratings and routing scores To improve routing for you Saved on your device using chrome.storage. This does not include the text of your prompts.

The extension does not read your conversation history, account details, email address, payment information, or cookies, and it does not access any website other than claude.ai.

Data collection and sharing

  • No collection. The extension does not send any data to the developer or to any third party.
  • No analytics or tracking. No analytics, telemetry, advertising, or crash-reporting tools are included.
  • No sale or transfer. Your data is never sold or transferred. It is not used for advertising, for creditworthiness or lending decisions, or for any purpose unrelated to the extension's single purpose.
  • No remote code. The extension does not load or run remotely hosted code.

When the extension switches your model, it does so by operating the claude.ai page you are already using. Your prompts are then sent to Anthropic by claude.ai itself, exactly as they would be without the extension. That transmission is governed by Anthropic's privacy policy, not by this one.

Permissions

The extension requests only what it needs for its single purpose:

  • Access to https://claude.ai/*: to read the prompt box and operate the model selector on claude.ai.
  • storage: to save your settings and ratings locally on your device.
  • activeTab to interact with the currently open claude.ai tab when you open the extension popup.

Data retention and deletion

All data the extension stores stays on your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the extension's data or by uninstalling the extension, which deletes everything it saved.

Security

Because your data is not transmitted off your device by the extension, the main protection is that it never leaves your browser. The source code is public at github.com/Mithsah1325/TokenSense-for-Claude, so you can check this for yourself.

Children's privacy

The extension does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Under laws such as the GDPR and CCPA, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal data held about you. Because the developer does not collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, delete, or export on the developer's side. If you have a question or request, contact the address below.

Changes to this policy
